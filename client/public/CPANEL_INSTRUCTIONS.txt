HOW TO DEPLOY ON CPANEL (public_html):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Login to your cPanel hosting account.
2. In the "Files" section, click on "File Manager".
3. Navigate into the "public_html" directory (or your subdomain directory).
4. Click "Upload" from the top menu toolbar.
5. Select and upload the zip file: "anubhuti-cpanel-public_html.zip".
6. Once uploaded (100% green bar), go back to File Manager.
7. Right-click on "anubhuti-cpanel-public_html.zip" and choose "Extract".
8. Extract it directly into "public_html".
9. (Optional) Make sure hidden files are visible in cPanel:
   - Click "Settings" (top right of File Manager)
   - Check "Show Hidden Files (dotfiles)"
   - Click Save
   - Verify that ".htaccess" is present in public_html.

10. Open your domain in any browser (e.g., https://yourdomain.com).
    Your website is now LIVE!
